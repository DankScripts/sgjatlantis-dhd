# Sliding Doors

Atlantis has hall, quarters, angular and wide-glass door kits, with multiple finishes. The kits place a multi-block doorway, rather than a single ordinary door.

## Placement

Most kits need a **4-block-wide by 4-block-high** clear frame. The wide-glass version needs a **5 x 4** frame and a **3 x 3** opening. Use the kit on the floor under the **left half** of a regular doorway; for wide glass, use the floor at the **center**. Face the direction in which you want to place it and clear the whole required volume.

Mount a separate door control panel on the **right jamb**, one block above the doorway floor, on each side you want to operate from. Use a working panel to toggle the door.

## Control crystals

Sneak-use an installed panel with an **empty hand** to remove its upper control crystal. Insert a **Door Control Crystal** into an empty or damaged panel to restore operation. If a door will not respond, check that the panel is correctly mounted and contains its crystal, and that the door frame is complete.
