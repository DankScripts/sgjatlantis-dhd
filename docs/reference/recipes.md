# Recipes and Items

Look in the **SGJ Atlantis** creative tab for available components. In survival, use an installed recipe viewer or the recipe book to check the recipes actually loaded by your game and datapacks.

The supplied Forge and NeoForge 2.0.5 source archives do not have identical recipe sets. Some primary device recipe files present in the Forge archive, including the Atlantis DHD, transporter room, hatch table, hatch panels and redstone port, are absent from the extracted NeoForge archive. Historical namespaces also appear in the Forge recipe files. A recipe file in an archive is not proof that its ingredients and result resolve successfully in a particular running game.

Do not assume that every creative-tab item is craftable on both loaders, or copy a recipe from another release without checking it in your installation. Operators can obtain creative items when appropriate; server rules and permissions still apply.

For ZPMs, Stargate crystals and cables, see Stargate Journey's own recipes and configuration. Lantea's native equipment belongs to its separate mod.
